## 3) Issues / Blockers

- Autonomous SmolVLA control, task success and saved demonstrations were not confirmed in the lab recap. Camera checks alone do not establish a working vision-to-action policy loop.
- The published SmolVLA timings used unlocked clocks, unlike the September locked-clock harness checks. Comparable sweep results still need controlled settings and saved JSON records; none from these SmolVLA runs are committed in `results/`.
- The synchronous loop pauses while planning each new 50-action chunk. A 1.129 s median planning time does not prove sustained, uninterrupted 30 Hz control or satisfy E-3's p99/deadline requirement.
- Board admin approvals, the final sweep power mode, native Linux/RoboTwin setup and the team's own arm order remain unconfirmed. The successful lab session does not establish those items as complete.
- Joint limits, velocity limits and watchdog behavior still need documented verification before autonomous physical trials (E-8).
