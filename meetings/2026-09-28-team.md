## 2) Accomplished During This Meeting

- Reviewed the first measurements. With default clock scaling, latency stayed flat from 112 to 672 px while board power rose from 6.5 W to 14.9 W, so the clock governor was hiding the extra work.
- **Decision:** lock the clocks (`jetson_clocks`) for every experiment. Re-ran the checks with clocks locked in all three power modes (12 records, [PR #4](https://github.com/Lambert-Nguyen/edge-vla-control-budget-jetson/pull/4)):
  - Worst-case (p99) latency at 224 px dropped from 4.9 ms to 1.4 ms.
  - Energy per step was about 150 mJ at 1344 px in every mode.
  - No throttling; the chip peaked at 61.5 °C.
- Hiwonder check (Lam): the kit uses Hiwonder HX-30HM and HX-10HM servos, not Feetech STS3215. This corrects what the team told Dr. Liu on 2026-09-22. Hiwonder's upstream LeRobot PRs (#3871, #3872) were closed without merging.
- **Decision:** hold the arm order until Dr. Liu weighs in. Feetech-based alternatives: WowRobo Package 3 ($299 + shipping), Seeed SO-ARM101 Pro kit ($277.99).
